Welcome to Polly the Polling Bot's User Guide!


Initiating the bot:
Typing /poll into your message space on Discord will initiate the bot

Inputting Poll Fields:
Two text boxes will appear on your message space, one next to topic, and one next to options. This is for you to fill out your fields for the poll you are starting

Fill out the title as a normal question, and then in the "options" box, type each option as you wish it to be displayed, followed by a comma ending each option
Putting a number of options outside the range of 2-10 will result in an error, as will not separating options by commas, requiring you to start from the beginning.

Starting the Poll:
Upon pressing enter the bot will initiate the poll with buttons for each of the inputs that were entered.
Clicking an option will result in a message that only you can see that says what you voted for.

After a period of time the poll will end and display the results of the poll, anonymously.
